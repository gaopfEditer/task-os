# Tokenomics

KGeN has two currencies:

* $rKGEN
* $KGEN

#### r-KGeN Token

The purpose of rKGEN is to reward operating nodes, users who have engaged with the KGeN Platform since inception, and businesses who have spent marketing dollars with KGeN.

rKGEN will eventually be converted into the KGeN token at a 1:1 ratio (subject to customisation) post-TGE.

Swapped rKGEN will be removed from circulation by invoking the burn function in the contract.

This approach helps manage market anticipation, counter derivative markets, reward early contributors, increase awareness around staking, and demonstrate ecosystem engagement to tier-1 exchanges.

#### $KGEN Token

$KGEN is the governance and utility token of the KGeN Protocol, and the primary token of the KGeN ecosystem. KCash serves as the platform currency and is redeemable through KStore while remaining convertible to $KGEN.

The network value of $KGEN grows as the KGeN Network expands through verified humans, Clans, developers, investors, brands, AI labs, and enterprise partners. Our core philosophy remains: "Come for the tools, stay for the network."

### $KGEN Tokenomics Levers

Three primary mechanisms drive the long-term value of $KGEN:

1. Supply Reduction
2. Staking
3. Product

#### Lever: Supply Reduction

The one-time Genesis Burn of 22M $KGEN, together with the perpetual revenue-funded supply reduction mechanism, permanently reduces circulating supply. As KGeN's AI business grows, buyback and retirement activity scales proportionally through transparent, on-chain execution.

#### Lever: Staking

$KGEN staking targets four key audiences:

Speculators

* Traders, investors, validators, and community members seeking long-term yield.

Businesses

* Teams seeking enhanced access and benefits for running campaigns on the KGeN Network.

Clans

* Larger Clan capacities.
* Reward multipliers based on Clan leaderboard standings.

Users

* Reward multipliers for campaigns and participation across the platform.

#### Lever: Product

$KGEN is the primary token of the KGeN ecosystem. AI labs, brands, developers, and ecosystem partners utilise KGeN products and services, while community members purchase rewards, subscriptions, digital assets, and other offerings.

Day-to-day platform transactions use KCash, the platform currency, which is redeemable through KStore and convertible to $KGEN
